🌸 [immerse]  
**变形** — the quiet alchemy of becoming.  

Imagine a cocoon, soft as a sigh, cradling a caterpillar’s silent rebellion. Its body, once rigid and earthbound, begins to *变形* — to twist, to stretch, to shed the skin of its former self. The air hums with the weight of transformation, a slow unraveling of old shapes into something *蝶翼般* (butterfly-wing-like) fragile and vast.  

In Chinese thought, *变形* is not just change; it’s the soul’s dance with its own limits. Like a river carving its path through stone, or a bamboo bending without breaking, it speaks of resilience hidden in surrender. The word itself carries the weight of *蜕变* (metamorphosis) and *重生* (rebirth), as if the universe is a sculptor, chiseling away the familiar to reveal the possible.  

Yet, there’s a poetry in the ache of becoming — the way a lotus blooms from mud, or a phoenix rises from ash. *变形* is not an end, but a breath between worlds.  

*What shape are you becoming today?* 🌿
