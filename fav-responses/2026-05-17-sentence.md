💫 **Key words to know:**  
- 既然 (since) – a bridge between cause and effect  
- 生命 (life) – the fleeting flame of existence  
- 终将 (will surely) – a promise of inevitability  
- 谢幕 (curtain down) – the final act of a performance  

**What the sentence says:**  
"Since life is destined to end, what is its purpose?"  

**Why it matters:**  
This line whispers a truth as old as time: life is a borrowed moment, a fleeting performance. It invites us 
to question whether our actions are written for the applause of eternity or the silence of oblivion. Like 
the bamboo that bends but never breaks, it challenges us to find meaning in the *无常* (impermanence) of 
existence.  

**Memory hook:**  
"Remember: Life's curtain falls, but its echoes linger."  

**Interactive close:**  
What would you do if you knew your time was limited? Would you still chase the *刹那* (moment) or build a *永
恒* (eternity) of impact? 🌿
