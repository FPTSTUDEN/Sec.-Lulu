武林中流传着一个故事
💫 **Key words to know:**  
- **武林 (wǔ lín)** - martial world (a metaphor for the world of martial arts and江湖)  
- **流传 (liú chuán)** - passed down (as in traditions, stories, or skills)  
- **故事 (gù shì)** - story (a narrative that carries meaning)  

**What the sentence says:**  
"A story that has been passed down in the martial world."  

**Simplified paraphrase:**  
"A tale that has been told for generations in the world of martial arts."  

**Why it matters:**  
This sentence captures the essence of how legends and values are preserved in the martial arts culture, 
blending history, heroism, and the spirit of *侠义* (chivalry).
